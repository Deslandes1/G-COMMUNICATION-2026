📱 Enable Notifications on iPhone

Notifications on iPhone require these steps:

1️⃣ Tap the Share button ⬆️ at the bottom of Safari

2️⃣ Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"

3️⃣ Tap Add in the top right

4️⃣ Close Safari, open JARVIS from your Home Screen

5️⃣ Go to Profile → Notifications → tap to enable

⚠️ Note: Notifications only work on iOS 16.4 or newer. Browsers cannot auto-allow — you must tap the button and confirm Apple's prompt.
