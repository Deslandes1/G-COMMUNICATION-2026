How it works

1. Share your ID from Profile.

2. The other person adds you as a contact.

3. Chat, send photos/videos/files, or voicemails — they sync via Firebase.

4. Voice calls require both people online at the same time.

5. A red badge shows unread messages — cleared when you open the chat.
